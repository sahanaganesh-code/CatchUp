**Hackathon Theme**: Health and Lifestyle

---

## The Problem

**26% of US adults have a disability** that affects meeting participation:
- Deaf/hard-of-hearing miss critical information
- People with ADHD struggle with note-taking while staying engaged
- Individuals with dyslexia or memory challenges can't retain spoken information
- Anxiety about missing details leads to stress and burnout

**Impact**: Exclusion from education, career opportunities, and social participation.

---

## Our Solution

**CatchUp** is an AI-powered meeting assistant that makes conversations accessible through:

1. **Real-Time Transcription** - Live captions for Zoom and in-person meetings
2. **Evidence-Based Q&A** - Ask questions, get answers with 2-5 timestamped quotes
3. **Intelligent Recaps** - Auto-generated summaries with key points
4. **Smart Actions** - Proposes tasks/events with approval gating

**Key Innovation**: Every answer includes verifiable evidence (no hallucination) - critical for trust in healthcare and education settings.

## Technology

- **Backend**: FastAPI + ChromaDB vector database + Google Gemini AI
- **Frontend**: Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS
- **AI**: Gemini embeddings for semantic search, Gemini LLM for grounded answers
- **Architecture**: Modular, scalable, production-ready

**Hard Rules**:
1. Every answer includes 2-5 evidence quotes (no hallucination)
2. Actions only execute with explicit approval (user control)
3. Modular architecture (maintainable and scalable)
